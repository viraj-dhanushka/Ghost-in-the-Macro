👻 Ghost in the Macro

Bring the business logic out of the spreadsheet and check it against the original.

Ghost reads the formulas in one Excel workbook, writes them as plain-English rules and a Python module, and compares that module with the workbook recalculated by LibreOffice. It is for analysts and developers who inherited a spreadsheet they cannot easily change or audit.
